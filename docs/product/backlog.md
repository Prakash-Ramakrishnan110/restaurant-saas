# ServeMyFood Product Backlog (Deferred Items)

The following items are intentionally excluded from the current release and tracked here with reasons:

1. **Online payments, UPI deep links & payment sound boxes**: Requires the restaurant owner's merchant payment gateway account and settlement KYC.
2. **Delivery, QR takeaway & scheduled pickup**: Requires customer phone/OTP verification.
3. **SMS and WhatsApp notifications**: Requires DLT template registration and per-message billing setup.
4. **Coupons, offers & tax-compliant digital receipts**: Tax formatting and promotional accounting rules are pending accountant confirmation.
5. **Customer accounts with OTP & server-side order history**: Deferred pending privacy legal review.
6. **Direct USB/LAN ESC/POS hardware bridge**: Browser CSS printing is active; native local bridge remains "Coming soon".
7. **Guest UI languages beyond English and Tamil**: Menu content translation prep is supported for 9 languages, while guest UI switcher awaits native-reviewed UI strings.
