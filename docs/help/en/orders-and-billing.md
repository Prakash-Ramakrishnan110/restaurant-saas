# Taking Orders, Kitchen Display & Billing

1. Use **{{nav:Floor POS}}** (or **{{nav:Counter Orders}}** for quick-service venues) to seat guests and send orders straight to the kitchen.
2. Kitchen staff track tickets on **{{nav:Kitchen KDS}}** and bump orders as **Preparing** and **Ready**.
3. Cashiers settle full payments under **{{nav:Cashier Billing}}** and close each shift under **{{nav:Shift Close}}**.
4. Note: All receipts in this build display `DEMO RECEIPT - NOT A TAX INVOICE` with no tax calculation lines.
