# Menu, Photos & Translations

1. Open **{{nav:Menu}}** to create categories and dishes with veg/non-veg/egg badges and prices in rupees.
2. Click **Upload photos** to batch-upload up to 50 dish photos (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) and review proposed matches before attaching.
3. Open any item to manage its 5-photo gallery, variants, option groups, ingredients note, and allergen note.
4. Open **{{nav:Translations}}** to prepare, review, and publish Tamil or other regional menu translations.
