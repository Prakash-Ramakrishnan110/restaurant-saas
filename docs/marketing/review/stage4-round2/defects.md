# Stage 4 — Visual & Accessibility Review Round 2 (`stage4-round2`)

Reviewed `1440x900` and `390x844` full-page captures (`home`, `home-ta`, `hardware`, `hardware-ta`, `tea-shops`, `tea-shops-ta`) and verified `metrics.json` (`0` axe violations, `0` overflows across `320px..1440px`).

## 16 Defects Identified & Fixed in Round 2 -> Round 3

1. **R2-01 — Common Rule 5 non-button orange icons (`VenuePage.tsx`, `DemoForm.tsx`)**: Replaced `text-[#FF6A00]` on `<Clock />` and `<Phone />` icons with `text-[#0B1F3B]` so `#FF6A00` is reserved strictly for brand mark and primary buttons with navy text.
2. **R2-02 — Form input heights and consent row gap (`DemoForm.tsx`, `home-390.png`, `home-1440.png`)**: Upgraded `#lead-name`, `#lead-phone`, `#lead-type`, and `#lead-city` from `h-10` (`40px`) to `h-12` (`48px`), enlarged checkbox to `h-5 w-5`, and gave the entire consent `<label>` `min-h-[48px]` instead of applying `min-h-[48px]` to the inline `"Privacy notice"` `<a>` (which created a tall vertical gap before the submit button).
3. **R2-03 — Mobile poster subtitle overlapped by centered Play button (`VideoSection.tsx`, `tea-shops-390.png`, `tea-shops-ta-390.png`)**: Moved the overlay Play CTA to the lower third (`items-end pb-4 sm:pb-6`) over a subtle bottom gradient so the poster headline and subtitle remain completely unobstructed at `320px` and `390px` in both English and Tamil.
4. **R2-04 — Clip H center overlay obscured `#76 NOW CALLING` hero token (`VenuePage.tsx`, `tea-shops-1440.png`, `tea-shops-390.png`)**: Repositioned `"Watch kitchen-to-TV clip"` to the bottom edge with a subtle bottom vignette so the live `#76` token callout stays crisp and readable before playback.
5. **R2-05 — Missing explicit `width` and `height` on `DeviceFrame` `<img>` elements (`DeviceFrame.tsx`)**: Added explicit `width`/`height` (`1280x800` for laptop/tablet, `540x900` for phone) and `fetchPriority="high"` when `eager` is true to eliminate CLS and satisfy Lighthouse image sizing rules.
6. **R2-06 — Missing bottom conversion CTA band on Venue pages (`VenuePage.tsx`, `tea-shops-1440.png`)**: Added a bilingual bottom CTA band (`Book a free demo` + `See hardware guide`) above the footer on `/tea-shops/`, `/restaurants/`, and `/hotels/` (with strictly zero `"table"` / `"மேஜை"` / `"மேசை"` words on tea-shops).
7. **R2-07 — Missing 3-step browser & TV setup strip + bottom CTA on `/hardware/` (`HardwarePage.tsx`, `hardware-1440.png`)**: Added a clear 3-step setup guide (1. Phone or tablet browser, 2. Optional TV browser link, 3. Optional 58mm/80mm browser print) and a bottom demo CTA card on `/hardware/` and `/ta/hardware/`.
8. **R2-08 — Focus ring contrast on dark navy sections & reduced-motion smooth scroll (`index.css`)**: Added high-contrast white `:focus-visible` outline inside dark `#0B1F3B` bands and disabled `scroll-behavior: smooth` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
9. **R2-09 — Missing `role="tabpanel"` and `aria-controls` on 5-step switcher (`HomePage.tsx`)**: Connected each `role="tab"` button (`id="step-tab-*"`, `aria-controls="step-tabpanel"`) to the step preview `role="tabpanel"` (`aria-labelledby`).
10. **R2-10 — Missing `aria-controls` on FAQ accordion items (`HomePage.tsx`)**: Linked each FAQ button (`id="faq-btn-*"`, `aria-controls="faq-panel-*"`) to its answer region (`id="faq-panel-*"`, `role="region"`).
11. **R2-11 — Tamil tax word (`வரி ரசீதுகள்`) & stale Coming-Soon copy in `copy.ts` + static check (`copy.ts`, `check_static.mjs`)**: Removed `"வரி ரசீதுகள்"` and outdated QR/stock coming-soon lines from `copy.ts`, and added Tamil `"வரி ரசீது"` / `"ஜிஎஸ்டி"` plus `"மேசை"` (on tea-shops) to `check_static.mjs`.
12. **R2-12 — Tight Tamil `h1`/`h2` line-height on `/ta/` pages (`HardwareSection.tsx`, `HomePage.tsx`, `hardware-ta-1440.png`)**: Relaxed Tamil display heading line-height to `leading-[1.28]` so Tamil vowel marks breathe cleanly across wrapped lines.
13. **R2-13 — English `"Call:"` / `"Phone:"` labels on Tamil routes and missing venue links in footer (`Layout.tsx`, `HomePage.tsx`)**: Localized `"Call:"` -> `"அழைக்க:"` and `"Phone:"` -> `"தொலைபேசி:"` in Tamil mode, and added `Cafes & restaurants` and `Hotels` links to the footer navigation.
14. **R2-14 — Small mobile brand logo at `390px` (`Layout.tsx`, `home-390.png`)**: Scaled header logo to `h-6 min-[380px]:h-7 sm:h-9` while preserving zero horizontal overflow at `320px`.
15. **R2-15 — Single-click playback start on lazy `<video>` elements (`VenuePage.tsx`, `VideoSection.tsx`)**: Added `useRef` + `useEffect` calling `v.load(); v.play().catch(() => {})` when the user clicks `"Watch kitchen-to-TV clip"` or `"Play demo video"` so video playback starts immediately on the first tap.
16. **R2-16 — Live `/api/v1/public/leads/` proxy in `vite preview` & Redis rate-limit reset (`vite.config.ts`, `scripts/marketing/verify.ps1`)**: Added `/api` proxy -> `http://127.0.0.1:8000` in `vite.config.ts` (`server` & `preview`) and updated `verify.ps1` to clear `:1:lead:ip:127.0.0.1` on `fusion_redis` with auth so Gate 4 submits against the real backend reliably.
